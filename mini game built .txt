Yes. Let's design it pin-to-pin, meaning we define exactly what is 3D, what is 2D, how they connect, how the camera works, how the ball moves, and how the UI sits on top.

For Auction XI, I would not make a traditional "2D game with some 3D objects." I would make a 2.5D broadcast cricket game:

3D world provides depth, players, ball, pitch, field and camera.
2D layers provide stadium atmosphere, HUD, field map, effects, menus and presentation.

The important thing is that the player should see one unified game, not notice the 2D/3D boundary.
